# Reporting {#reporting}

Operational reporting for supervisors and administrators.

## Dashboard {#reporting.dashboard}

A single view of open cases, SLA health, and employee workload.

### Requirements

- Filter by team, priority, and date range.
- SLA health shows counts for on-track, at-risk, and breached cases.

## Scheduled Exports {#reporting.exports}

Automatic CSV exports delivered on a schedule.

### Requirements

- Exports can be scheduled daily or weekly.
- Recipients are configured per export.
- Failed exports retry twice before notifying an administrator.
